## Description

**Human decision (Nik, 2026-10-30):** Muse audited the live public pages for AI-writing tropes at Nik's request; Nik approved Muse's suggested edits in chat and directed: file this ticket and implement the fixes.

**Audit verdict:** the vocabulary layer is clean estate-wide (no delve/leverage/seamless/unlock filler, no "in today's fast-paced world"). The tells are rhythmic and structural: slogan triplets, abstract-noun triads, unanchored comparatives, single-metaphor repetition ("bridge"), staccato fragment headings, not-X-but-Y antithesis, and em-dash chaining. The counterweight already present on every page — and explicitly NOT to be edited — is the house disclaimer discipline ("A possible handoff, not a client result"; "These are prior roles and account experience, not Teambotics client engagements"; "illustrative examples, not transcripts"; "Prototype only… does not ingest real email").
