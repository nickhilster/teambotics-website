## Description

**Human decision (Nik, 2026-10-26):** Adopt the company-wide Teambotics tagline **“Learn. Commit. Push.”** Exact punctuation, capitalization, order, and English wording matter. This is not a Git slogan or a claim that Teambotics only builds software. “Learn” is intentionally human-first; the Git interpretation of commit/push is a secondary layer. The quiet inspiration is *Jai Bhim* and the emancipatory spirit of “Educate. Agitate. Organize.” This connection should be preserved in internal brand provenance, without imposing an explanatory paragraph under every public appearance.

**Canonical decision:** [Teambotics company brand](https://github.com/nickhilster/Teambotics/blob/main/company/brand.md). **Cross-repo coordination:** [Hangar rollout](https://github.com/nickhilster/hangar/blob/main/plans/2026-10-26-teambotics-tagline-rollout.md).

### Website work
- [ ] Make “Learn. Commit. Push.” visibly legible as the primary company tagline on the homepage, while keeping the service proposition distinct.
- [ ] Reflect the tagline consistently in global header/footer or other appropriate brand lockups and metadata, mobile + desktop, light + dark themes.
- [ ] Preserve the exact English tagline on English, French Canadian, and Latin American Spanish variants; localize accompanying explanatory copy, not the branded line.
- [ ] Audit About, Manifesto, SEO/structured data, social previews, blog and marketing pages for contradictions and opportunities to align; do not erase useful, truthful service descriptions.
- [ ] Review copy for accessibility, responsive line wrapping, semantics, and translations.
- [ ] Run pnpm translations:check, lint, typecheck, test, build; review on testing.teambotics.app before publishing production.
- [ ] Confirm the live www.teambotics.app site reflects the approved line after production release.

### Company-wide rollout (tracked centrally)

- [ ] Audit sales/proposal decks, templates, outreach, email signatures, applications, social bios, public company profiles, LinkedIn, blog signatures, and common channel assets.
- [ ] Human review of external communications that carry the new line.
