## Description
**Origin:** Mobile layout audit of the live site teambotics.app, requested by Nik (2026-11-09) and run via Symphony. All 13 pages were rendered in real Chrome at 390px and inspected by eye. Full report: `docs/mobile-layout-audit-2026-11-09.md`. This also closes out the open "check the mobile layout ... after the chat-control fix" follow-up noted in TBW-001.

**Verdict:** the site holds up well on a phone. No sideways scrolling, no clipped or broken text, no broken images at 390px across all 13 pages. Two real issues remain, both site-wide, plus minor items.

### Issues
- [ ] **Floating "Chat" button overlaps page content (notable).** The pink pill fixed at the right edge sits on top of body text on `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/land-acknowledgment` and `/about`, over the hero image on `/everythings-computer`, and on or beside the hero buttons ("Open Product" / "Discuss ..." / "All products") on every product page (`ryfine`, `ltb-buddy`, `redactorbuddy`, `mdownmanager`, `code2motion`, `easybuddy`, `storytellr`). It covers text on scroll and sits close enough to the hero buttons to risk blocking taps. Fix: reposition or shrink the button on mobile so it does not overlap content or interactive controls.
- [ ] **Header controls are tiny tap targets (notable).** Measured: menu toggle ~20x36px, theme toggle ~19x36px, the header "..." links ~17x30px, footer credit links ~19-21px tall. Target is 44x44px. Fix: enlarge hit areas to ~44px across the header controls and footer credit links.
- [ ] **No visible call to action in the phone header (minor).** The nav collapses into the menu button, so "Contact" lives only inside the menu. The hero "Discuss your workflow" button still provides a path. Fix (optional): add a visible Contact action to the phone header.

### Minor / to verify manually
- [ ] `/privacy` "Third-Party Services" table flagged as a scrollable region; confirm it does not scroll sideways on a phone (could not confirm from the thumbnail).
- [ ] `/everythings-computer`: at 200% text zoom the page scrolls sideways by ~39px from the press pledge section. Normal text size is fine.
- [ ] Home page: small 12px mono labels have low contrast (measured 3.63:1 vs 4.5:1 target); the privacy link in the form note is not visually distinguishable from surrounding text.

### Scope / limits
- Rendered at 390px only. 360px and 414px not yet checked.
- Open mobile menu not rendered, so menu-overlap is untested.
- Tap-target sizes, contrast, and the 200% overflow are measured by the checker, not seen by eye.
- Non-mobile checker failures (missing badge, og tags, canonical links, image CDN, 307-on-unknown) were excluded as not applicable to this Vercel-deployed site.

### Acceptance
- [ ] Chat button no longer overlaps content or hero buttons at 390/360/414px.
- [ ] Header controls and footer credit links meet ~44x44px tap targets.
- [ ] `/privacy` table and `/everythings-computer` 200%-zoom overflow verified or fixed.
