## Description
**Origin:** Mobile layout audit of the live site teambotics.app, requested by Nik (2026-11-09) and run via Symphony. All 13 pages were rendered in real Chrome at 390px and inspected by eye. Full report: `docs/mobile-layout-audit-2026-11-09.md`. This also closes out the open "check the mobile layout ... after the chat-control fix" follow-up noted in TBW-001.

**Verdict:** the site holds up well on a phone. No sideways scrolling, no clipped or broken text, no broken images at 390px across all 13 pages. Two real issues remain, both site-wide, plus minor items.

### Issues
- [x] **Floating "Chat" button overlaps page content (notable).** The pink pill fixed at the right edge sits on top of body text on `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/land-acknowledgment` and `/about`, over the hero image on `/everythings-computer`, and on or beside the hero buttons ("Open Product" / "Discuss ..." / "All products") on every product page (`ryfine`, `ltb-buddy`, `redactorbuddy`, `mdownmanager`, `code2motion`, `easybuddy`, `storytellr`). It covers text on scroll and sits close enough to the hero buttons to risk blocking taps. Fix: reposition or shrink the button on mobile so it does not overlap content or interactive controls.
- [x] **Header controls are tiny tap targets (notable).** Measured: menu toggle ~20x36px, theme toggle ~19x36px, the header "..." links ~17x30px, footer credit links ~19-21px tall. Target is 44x44px. Fix: enlarge hit areas to ~44px across the header controls and footer credit links.
- [ ] **No visible call to action in the phone header (minor).** **State confirmed 2026-11-10 at 390px: the header bar shows logo, language select (108×44) and menu toggle (44×44) only; Contact exists solely inside the collapsed menu. This is a design decision for Nik, not a defect — the hero CTA remains the visible path.** The nav collapses into the menu button, so "Contact" lives only inside the menu. The hero "Discuss your workflow" button still provides a path. Fix (optional): add a visible Contact action to the phone header.

### Minor / to verify manually
- [x] `/privacy` "Third-Party Services" table flagged as a scrollable region; confirm it does not scroll sideways on a phone (could not confirm from the thumbnail). **Verified 2026-11-10: the page itself never scrolls sideways at 390/360/414px (document width = viewport width at all three); the table is a contained scroll region by design (`display:block; overflow-x:auto` in `.legal-markdown table`) — its content is 567px wide inside a ~316px frame at 390px. Closed as confirmed-contained, not a page defect.**
- [ ] `/everythings-computer`: at 200% text zoom the page scrolls sideways by ~39px from the press pledge section. Normal text size is fine. **Reproduced 2026-11-10 under the audit's apparent conditions (200% root text size at phone width): exactly 39px of overflow at 390px (document 429px). Culprits measured: `.site-header__meta` / `.site-nav-toggle` and the press-pledge intro block (`.pressPledgeIntro` / `.overline`), both extending past the right edge. No overflow at 640px+ under the same text size, and none under true 2x browser page-zoom at 1280px. Confirmed, NOT fixed — left open deliberately pending a fix decision.**
- [ ] Home page: small 12px mono labels have low contrast (measured 3.63:1 vs 4.5:1 target); the privacy link in the form note is not visually distinguishable from surrounding text. **Confirmed 2026-11-10 by direct measurement: the numbered mono labels (01–06, 12px, `#6f8978`) measure 3.64:1 on the paper background — the audit's 3.63:1 reproduced; mono eyebrows (`#617669`) measure 4.37:1 and 4.14:1, also under 4.5:1. The form-note "Privacy Policy" link is `text-decoration: none` and differs from surrounding text only by a subtle colour step (`#315642` vs `#4d6256`). Confirmed, NOT fixed — a token-level darkening of the two sage tones is the candidate fix, pending Nik's call.**

### Scope / limits
- Rendered at 390px only. 360px and 414px not yet checked.
- Open mobile menu not rendered, so menu-overlap is untested.
- Tap-target sizes, contrast, and the 200% overflow are measured by the checker, not seen by eye.
- Non-mobile checker failures (missing badge, og tags, canonical links, image CDN, 307-on-unknown) were excluded as not applicable to this Vercel-deployed site.

### Acceptance
- [x] Chat button no longer overlaps content or hero buttons at 390/360/414px.
- [x] Header controls and footer credit links meet ~44x44px tap targets.
- [ ] `/privacy` table and `/everythings-computer` 200%-zoom overflow verified or fixed.
