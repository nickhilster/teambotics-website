What we do

Operational AI for environments that most vendors avoid.

Most AI tools are built for controlled environments with clean data and forgiving error tolerance. We build for the opposite — the regulated, the operational, the frontline. Environments where failure has a real cost.

That means different architecture decisions, different deployment strategies, and a different posture toward ongoing maintenance. We don't hand off prototypes. We maintain operational software that teams rely on.

"Disciplined intelligence expressed through restraint" — that's the design principle for everything we build. Powerful, precise, and never in the way.
