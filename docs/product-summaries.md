## LTB Buddy

**One-sentence summary**

LTB Buddy is a guided workflow tool that helps users navigate landlord-tenant board processes and documentation.

**Longer summary**

LTB Buddy assists individuals and organizations involved in landlord-tenant board proceedings. It provides structured, step-by-step guidance through common processes, helps users understand required documentation, and surfaces relevant information in a clear and organized way. It is designed for people who need to navigate a complex administrative process without necessarily having legal expertise.

LTB Buddy is a workflow and information tool. It is not a legal service and does not provide legal advice.

**Audience**

Individuals, property managers, and organizations navigating landlord-tenant board processes who need structured, accessible guidance rather than an open-ended search experience.

**Workflow problem**

Landlord-tenant board processes involve specific forms, timelines, and procedural steps that are difficult to navigate without institutional knowledge. The information exists but is dispersed, inconsistently formatted, and hard to act on — especially for people without legal training.

**How Teambotics frames this product**

LTB Buddy is a compliance-aware interface built for a regulated, high-stakes operational context. It reduces the friction of navigating a structured process by making the relevant information findable and actionable, without replacing the judgment of qualified professionals.

**What the product should not be claimed to do**

- LTB Buddy does not provide legal advice.
- LTB Buddy does not represent users in any legal proceeding.
- LTB Buddy does not guarantee outcomes in any landlord-tenant matter.
- LTB Buddy does not replace a lawyer or qualified legal professional.
- Do not claim specific case success rates, resolution outcomes, or user volume.

**Chatbot-safe answer**

> LTB Buddy is a Teambotics product that helps people navigate landlord-tenant board workflows and documentation. It provides structured guidance for common processes in that domain. It is an information and workflow tool — it does not give legal advice and is not a substitute for a qualified legal professional. Anyone with a legal matter should consult a lawyer.
