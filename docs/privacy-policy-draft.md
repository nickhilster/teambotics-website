## Overview

Teambotics ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at teambotics.app (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit the Site, submit a lead form, or interact with our chatbot assistant.

We keep this policy as plain as possible. If you have questions, contact us at hello@teambotics.app.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide Directly

**Lead form submissions.** When you submit the contact or lead form on the Site, we collect the fields you complete: name, email address, organization, interest area, and message. Submission is voluntary.

**Chatbot interactions.** If you use the chatbot assistant on the Site, your typed messages are processed to generate a response. We may log those interactions for quality and safety review. Do not enter personal, financial, medical, legal, or confidential information into the chatbot.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to collect anonymized, aggregate data about how visitors use the Site. This may include pages visited, referral source, browser type, device type, and general geographic region. This data does not include personally identifiable information in a form that identifies individual visitors.

**Cookies and local storage.** The Site may use cookies or browser storage for functional purposes, such as remembering your theme preference. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If we add analytics or marketing cookies in the future, this policy will be updated before they are deployed.

## How We Use Information

Information collected through the Site is used to:

- Respond to lead form inquiries and follow up on expressed interest.
- Operate and improve the chatbot assistant.
- Understand how the Site is used so we can improve it.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

---

## How Information Is Stored

**Hosting.** The Site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel processes traffic and may store server-side logs in connection with site operation. See Vercel's privacy documentation for details on their data practices.

**Lead form data.** Submitted form data is stored in a Neon-hosted PostgreSQL database. Access is restricted. We take reasonable steps to protect data in transit and at rest.

**Chatbot processing.** When the chatbot feature is active, your messages may be sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI processes inputs under their own terms of service and privacy policy. We do not use chatbot interactions to train OpenAI's models under our current agreement.
