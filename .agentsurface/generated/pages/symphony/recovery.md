# When the conversation misses, make the next move smaller.

These short prompts help you correct direction, restore missing context, or pause before an unclear action.

Scope: These are illustrative situations and user practices, not a report of observed Symphony failures, their frequency, or a diagnosis of internal causes. Adapt each prompt to the conversation in front of you.

01 / RECOVERY
## The answer is too generic

When: It could apply to almost anyone, or it optimizes for the wrong outcome.

TRY THIS
“You assumed ____. For this task, success means ____. Reframe your recommendation around that. Label any assumptions you still need to make.”

Stop check: If the recommendation still misses, add one concrete example of the situation before asking again.

02 / RECOVERY
## A guess is presented like a fact

When: The response sounds certain, but you cannot see what source supports it.

TRY THIS
“Separate what you know from what you infer. For each important claim, show the source or mark it unknown. Do not fill gaps with a confident guess.”

Stop check: Do not use an unverified claim to make an external or consequential decision.

03 / RECOVERY
## Important context is missing

When: The suggestion ignores a constraint, audience, deadline, or fact you meant to include.

TRY THIS
“Add this missing context: ____. Keep these constraints: ____. Please update the recommendation and point out any conflict with what you said earlier.”

Stop check: If the missing fact changes the decision, restate the goal and constraints before continuing.

04 / RECOVERY
## The source of truth may have changed

When: Two notes, records, or instructions disagree.

TRY THIS
“These sources conflict: ____ and ____. Do not choose silently. Tell me what each source says, which one you propose to use, and what you need me to confirm.”

Stop check: Pause any action that depends on the conflict until the owner confirms which source governs.

05 / RECOVERY
## The next action is unclear

When: A response says it is ready to act, but the exact step, destination, resource cost, or approval is vague.

TRY THIS
“Do not proceed yet. Describe the exact proposed action, destination, expected external effect, and any resource cost. Mark anything you cannot verify as unknown, then wait for my decision.”

Stop check: An explanation or draft is not permission to act. Decide only after the specific proposal is clear.

06 / RECOVERY
## A handoff lost a constraint

When: A prepared task or response omitted a requirement from the original request.

TRY THIS
“The handoff missed this constraint: ____. Compare the current brief with the original request, list what changed or disappeared, and revise the brief. Do not execute it.”

Stop check: Review the corrected handoff yourself before it is used by another person or process.

REUSABLE TASK BRIEF

## Add enough context before you hand work off.

Use this when a task may run without a live back-and-forth. It is a general writing aid, not a claim that every Symphony workflow is headless or needs a long brief.

Goal: What outcome do I need?
Context: What facts and sources should guide the work?
Constraints: What must be included, avoided, or kept unchanged?
Output: What should the result look like?
Unknowns: What must be asked or labeled instead of guessed?
Boundary: What should be prepared only, and what must not be done?
Stop condition: When should the work pause for my decision?
For an interactive task, invite a clarifying question instead of overfilling the brief. For work that proceeds without follow-up, verify what context the workflow can actually access.
