# Three questions before you hand work off.

Resource use, outside effects, and permission are related, but they are not the same thing. Check each one separately before a task moves beyond conversation.

Independent Teambotics guidance. This is a user checklist, not a description or guarantee of Symphony’s current controls or billing behavior.

THE MAP / THREE INDEPENDENT LENSES

## One request can raise one, two, or all three questions.

Do not infer cost from an external effect, or infer permission from a cost label. Find the current answer for the specific action.

01 / Resource
### Could this use credits or another limited resource?

Look for current, task-specific cost information. If it is unavailable, mark the cost unknown and ask before proceeding.

02 / Outside effect
### Could this reach another person or change something outside the conversation?

Name the destination and the exact change. Treat a draft, a proposed action, and a completed action as different states.

03 / Permission + owner
### Who owns the decision, and what approval is needed?

Keep the human decision visible. If scope, destination, cost, or evidence changes, review the decision again.

STOP CONDITIONS

## Unknown means pause.

01

### You cannot tell what it will cost

Pause. Ask for an estimate or state plainly that the cost is unknown.

02

### You cannot tell what will change

Ask for the exact proposed action, destination, and expected effect.

03

### The source or owner is unclear

Clarify which information governs and who has authority to decide.

04

### The action is hard to reverse

Keep it at the discussion or draft stage until a person reviews the consequences.
