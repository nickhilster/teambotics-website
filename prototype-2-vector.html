Positioning

Not AI in general. AI for the hard ones.

Where other AI vendors optimize for the easy case, we start from the constraint.

Most AI tools assume a controlled environment. Teambotics builds for the opposite: the regulated, the operational, the frontline.

We work in environments where AI systems must meet compliance standards, integrate with existing institutional workflows, and remain reliable under pressure. That means a different approach to architecture, to deployment, and to the ongoing relationship with the systems we ship.

We don't hand off prototypes. We maintain operational software — systems that clinical HR teams, shift supervisors, and operational leads depend on daily.

Capabilities

What we build.

01
Regulated Environment AI
Compliance-first systems for HIPAA, FMLA, ADA, and sector-specific frameworks. Auditability and traceability are architectural, not afterthoughts. We work in the space most AI vendors avoid.
02
Operational Intelligence
AI that surfaces the right information at the right moment for frontline and operational teams — decision support built for high-pressure, time-sensitive environments where delay has a cost.

Workflow Automation

How We Work

Four phases. One continuous relationship.

01

Discover

We embed in the operational context first. Understand constraints, workflows, and failure modes before writing a line of code. The environment drives the architecture.

02

Design

Architecture and interaction design that respects regulatory constraints and the reality of how people actually work under pressure — not how they're supposed to.

03

Deploy

Phased rollout with structured feedback loops. Production systems with real monitoring — not soft launches that quietly go unmaintained.

04

Evolve

Operational AI isn't a one-time build. We maintain and develop systems over time as environments, regulations, and organizational needs change.
