Normalization: All amounts are reported in USD where a provider's list pricing is USD-denominated; no currency conversion was performed since no non-USD costs were identified.

Inaccessible in this session: Vercel billing/usage dashboard, Neon console and billing export, OpenAI usage dashboard, GitHub billing page, domain registrar records, any invoice or bank/card statement.

Pricing sources: No official pricing page was cited with a specific dollar figure in this report, because no usage volume was available to apply pricing to — citing a rate without a quantity would create false precision.
